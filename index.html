Advanced columns are actual 2025–26 regular-season values. Rate values are per game or per 48 minutes as labeled. eFG% = effective field-goal percentage, computed as (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA. AST RATIO, AST/TO, TO RATIO, STL/TO, NBA Rating, plus-minus, and points per estimated possession are ESPN-reported statistics. Missing or zero-denominator metrics display as —.
OFFENSE · VOLUME + EFFICIENCYOffense weights scoring volume most: PTS/G 35%; AST/G and 3PM/G 10% each; TS% 15%; eFG% 10%; 3P%, assist ratio, AST/TO, and inverse TO ratio 5% each. This keeps a high-volume scorer from being buried by several overlapping shooting percentages.
DEFENSE · 5 BOX-SCORE SIGNALSDefense gives equal weight to STL/G, BLK/G, defensive REB/G, total REB/G, and STL/TO. It uses per-game impact rather than per-48 rates, which can exaggerate short-minute roles. These are box-score indicators, not opponent tracking.
OVERALL · OUR RANKINGOnly players with at least 20 NBA games are ranked. Overall score = 45% offense + 30% defense + 25% PER, with all component percentiles compared only among qualifying players. This is a per-game impact model, not an official NBA or ESPN rating.

Metrics behind the projections
The stats that matter for smarter fantasy decisions.
01
Box-score production
Points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, 3-pointers, turnovers, field-goal percentage, and free-throw percentage form the standard 9-cat foundation.
02
Efficiency & volume
FGM/FGA and FTM/FTA show the volume behind percentages. True shooting, usage rate, and per-minute rates add context to scoring and role.
03
Role & opportunity
Minutes, starts, rotation changes, touches, and assist opportunities help identify rising or declining roles and breakout potential.
04
Availability & schedule
Projected games, injury history, rest risk, and weekly team schedules affect season totals and head-to-head lineup value.
05
Fantasy context
Position eligibility, replacement-level value, category scarcity, turnovers, league scoring, and roster fit shape ranks, draft targets, and trade impact.
